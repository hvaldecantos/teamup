## New Scorer:

The main idea in this scrorer is to use all attributes from the entites to compute the score of a team.

### How the Idea Works

* **Standardize Data**: If Characteristic 1 is "Weight in kg" (range 50-100) and Characteristic 2 is "pH level" (range 6-8), the weight will overpower the pH. Convert all values to a scale of 0 to 1 first.

* **Define Equal**: equal means the groups have the same average (means).

* **The score**: compute the score a de difference between the means of the groups.
